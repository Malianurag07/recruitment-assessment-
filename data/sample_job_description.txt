Job Title: AI/ML Engineer (Entry Level)

About the role
We are building AI-powered products and are looking for an AI/ML Engineer to join our team. You will
develop machine learning models, expose them through APIs, and help deploy them to production.

Requirements
- Strong Python programming skills
- Hands-on experience with Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch
- Experience building REST APIs with FastAPI or Flask
- Working knowledge of SQL databases
- Familiarity with Docker and Git
- Experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and RAG

Nice to have
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS)
- Knowledge of vector databases
- Computer vision experience (OpenCV)

Experience: 0-2 years. Freshers with strong projects or internships are welcome.
We value clear communication and teamwork.
